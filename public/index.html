Getting started

A simple checklist for your brick collection including LEGO and a growing list of other brick brands. It’s easy to get started and you only need an email to sign up to save your collection and have it on every device.

  1. Pick a brand or themeTap a theme at the top, use the filters below, or search by name, set number, subtheme or tag.
  2. Select “I own it”Select “I own it” (the box beside a set) to add it to your collection. Tap its condition to set new, used or incomplete and how many copies.
  3. Star what you wantStarred sets go on your Wanted list with prices and Buy buttons. Export any list as a spreadsheet.

        Listing details

        BrickLink store upload

        Turns your sale or trade list into a BrickLink upload file, one lot for each copy (identical copies are put together). Each copy goes to your stockroom (hidden until you check it) or on sale now, as chosen in its Listing details.

          1. Download the file below (or copy it).
          2. On BrickLink, open Upload inventory ↗ and choose the file, or paste it into the upload box.
          3. Check the lots on BrickLink’s verify page, then submit.

          Share your sale or trade list

          Post your whole list in one go. Instead of a separate post for every set, share one link in Facebook groups, on Instagram or in a message. It opens a page with everything you’re selling or trading, any brand, with pictures, condition, details and your prices.

          How it works
          1. Mark what you’re letting go. Click a set’s condition chip and choose how many copies are for sale or trade. Add details and your own price under Listing details if you like.
          2. Say how buyers reach you below, then create your link.
          3. Share it. Paste the link, or Copy a post with your list already written, into Facebook groups or anywhere.
          4. Buyers message you directly. You agree payment and postage between you: BrickCollect isn’t part of the sale.
          5. Hold something back. Set a copy to Unlisted in its Listing details (e.g. it’s reserved) and it stays on your Sale or trade list but leaves the page. Set it back to Listed any time.
          6. It keeps itself up to date. Sold something? Mark it Sold or traded and it drops off the page. The same link keeps working.

          Sign up free to share your list. Your link needs an account, so it keeps working and updates when your list changes.
