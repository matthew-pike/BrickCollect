Welcome to BrickCollect

Built by an enthusiast, for enthusiasts. BrickCollect covers LEGO and a growing list of other brick brands, starting with LumiBricks and Pantasy. The goal is simple: make everything easy to find in a few clicks, on a clean, fast, easy-to-read site, with the ability to export your collection. Manage your collection, track the sets and minifigures you still need, and see every theme's timeline in one screen. It’s free, and you can sign in to keep your collection on every device.

It’s also a quick way to value your sets when you’re selling, insuring or trading. Tick what you own and BrickCollect adds up what it’s worth, used and new. Enter a percentage in the bar below to see what you’d ask, or offer, at that share of market value.

To get started, select a brand or theme below, or search above.
