About BrickCollect

BrickCollect is a free collection tracker for LEGO and a growing list of other brick brands. Tick the sets you own, keep a Wanted list, see what your collection is worth, and share or sell it, all in one place.

Open My collection Browse all sets

What you can do

  • Track your sets and minifigures. Tick what you own, with condition and how many copies. You see your sets, pieces and minifigures add up, and how close you are to finishing a theme.
  • Keep a Wanted list. Star the sets you want and buy from LEGO, BrickLink or the brand’s own store when you’re ready.
  • Know what it’s worth. Values from BrickLink prices, in your own currency, new or used.
  • Know before it’s gone. Retired and retiring-soon sets are marked, and you can filter by them.
  • Every set in story order. Timelines for Star Wars, Marvel, Harry Potter, Lord of the Rings and more.
  • Your own tags. On display, In storage, Built, Still boxed: anything you like. Only you see them.
  • See your collection at a glance by brand and theme, and share it to your Instagram Story as a picture.
  • Sell or trade. Add listing details to each copy, make a file for a BrickLink store, or share one link to your whole sale list in Facebook groups.
  • Bring your collection. Import from Rebrickable, Brickset, BrickLink or a spreadsheet, and export any list.

Free, and no password needed

You can start ticking straight away. To keep your collection safe and see it on your phone and computer, sign up free with your email (we send you a link, no password) or Google. BrickCollect is open now in beta, so things are still being added and improved.

Who makes it

BrickCollect is an Art of Bricks project, made in Sydney, Australia by a collector who wanted one simple place for LEGO and the other brick brands on the shelf.

Where the information comes from

The LEGO catalog comes from Rebrickable and is refreshed every week. Prices come from BrickLink. Other brands are listed by hand from public set information. Some store links are affiliate links: if you buy through them, The Art of Bricks may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It helps keep BrickCollect free.
