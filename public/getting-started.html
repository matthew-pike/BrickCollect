Getting started

A simple checklist for your brick collection including LEGO and a growing list of other brick brands. It’s easy to get started and you only need an email to sign up to save your collection and have it on every device.

Open My collection Browse all sets

1. Pick a brand or theme

In My collection, tap a theme at the top, use the filters, or search by name, set number, subtheme or tag. You can paste several set numbers into the search to see just those sets.

2. Select “I own it”

Select “I own it” (the box beside a set) to add it to your collection. Tap its condition to set new, used or incomplete, and how many copies you have. Minifigures that come with your sets count as owned too, and you can add loose ones on the Minifigures tab.

3. Star what you want

Starred sets go on your Wanted list with prices and Buy buttons for LEGO, BrickLink or the brand’s own store. Pick a theme to see what’s left to complete it.

4. Save your collection

You can add up to 10 sets and minifigures without an account. Sign up free with your email or Google to keep going, keep your collection safe and see it on your phone and computer.

Already tracking your sets somewhere else?

Use Import in My collection to bring them in from Rebrickable, Brickset, BrickLink or a spreadsheet. Export gives you any list as a spreadsheet.

There’s more when you’re ready

  • Timeline: sets from your favourite films and shows in story order.
  • Retiring soon: a coral RS marks sets leaving shops; a grey R means retired.
  • Your own tags: On display, In storage, Built, Still boxed, or anything you like.
  • Collection insights: your collection by brand and theme, and a picture to share on Instagram.
  • Sell or trade: see what your collection is worth, list copies with details, share one link to your whole list, or make a file for a BrickLink store.
