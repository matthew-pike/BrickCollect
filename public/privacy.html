Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

BrickCollect is a free collection tracker made by The Art of Bricks. This page explains what we keep and why, in plain terms. Questions? Email brickcollectapp@gmail.com.

Using BrickCollect without an account

You can try BrickCollect with up to 10 sets and minifigures without an account, then sign up to save more. If you don’t sign in, the ones you add with “I own it” (with their condition, copies and any marked for sale or trade), your wanted list, your currency, price location and display settings are stored only in your own browser. We don’t receive them. Clearing your browser’s data removes them, so use Export → My collection now and then as a backup.

If you sign in

  • Your email address, so we can send your sign-in link and recognise you. If you use Google, we receive your email address from Google; we don’t get your Google password.
  • Your collection: the sets and minifigures you’ve ticked, their condition and copies, any marked for sale or trade, your wanted list and any prices you’ve entered yourself. Only you can see it.
  • Your price location (for example Australia), so the twice-daily price refresh keeps prices for that location up to date.

We use these only to run BrickCollect. We never sell them or share them with anyone.

If you share your sale or trade list

Sharing is off unless you turn it on (Export → Share your sale or trade list). When it’s on, anyone with your link can see a page with the copies on your Sale or trade list (set, brand, picture, condition, your details for buyers and price) and whatever you chose to show: a name, where you are, your Instagram and another way to reach you. Your email address, private notes and stockroom choices are never shown. The page is kept out of search engines unless you turn on Let Google and other search engines find my page; then people searching for a set can find it. You can turn that off again at any time. Stop sharing deletes the page straight away, and deleting your account deletes it too. BrickCollect doesn’t take part in any sale: buyers and sellers deal directly.

Who stores it

BrickCollect runs on Google’s Firebase: it hosts the site, handles sign-in (including sending sign-in emails) and stores accounts and collections in Sydney, Australia. A copy is backed up daily and backups are kept for 7 days.

LEGO set images come from Rebrickable and other brands’ images from their own online stores, prices from BrickLink, exchange rates from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter, and fonts from Google Fonts. These services see your device’s internet address when your browser loads their content, as with any website.

Buy links

The small buttons that take you to a brand’s store (such as LumiBricks and Pantasy) may be affiliate links: if you buy something after following one, BrickCollect may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. It helps keep BrickCollect free. We don’t receive your details or what you bought, only that a sale came through the link.

Counting visits

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of BrickCollect people use, such as how many people visit each day, from which countries and devices, and which features they use (for example that a set was ticked, a theme picked, a search made or a list exported, and roughly how big collections are). It never receives which sets you own. It sets small cookies to tell new visitors from returning ones. It doesn’t receive your name, email or collection, and we’ve switched off all of its advertising features. Visitors in the UK, Europe and Switzerland are asked first, and nothing is counted with cookies until they say OK.

Cookies and browser storage

Besides Google Analytics above, BrickCollect uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in, to remember your settings, and to keep a copy of the site and your saved collection on your device so it opens offline (for example, when you’ve installed it to your home screen). It doesn’t use advertising cookies. If that changes, we’ll update this page and ask for your consent first.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself: sign in, open your account (top right) and choose Delete my account. Your account and the collection saved to it are deleted straight away, and disappear from the daily backups within 7 days. You can keep a copy of your collection in your browser if you like. If you can’t sign in, email brickcollectapp@gmail.com from the address you used and we’ll remove it.
