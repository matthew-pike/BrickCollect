Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

BrickCollect is a free collection tracker run by The Art of Bricks. This page explains what we keep and why, in plain terms.

Using BrickCollect without an account

If you don’t sign in, the sets and minifigures you tick, your currency and your display settings are stored only in your own browser. We don’t receive them. Clearing your browser’s data removes them, so use Export my collection now and then as a backup.

If you sign in

  • Your email address, so we can send your sign-in link and recognise you. If you use Google, we receive your email address from Google; we don’t get your Google password.
  • Your collection: the sets and minifigures you’ve ticked and any prices you’ve entered yourself. Only you can see it.
  • Price sync use: how many prices you’ve synced today, so we can share BrickLink’s daily limit fairly.

We use these only to run BrickCollect. We don’t sell them or share them with advertisers.

Who stores it

Accounts and collections are stored with Supabase, and the site is hosted by Netlify. Set images come from Rebrickable, prices from BrickLink, exchange rates from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter, and fonts from Google Fonts. These services see your device’s internet address when your browser loads their content, as with any website.

Cookies

BrickCollect uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings. It doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, for example if we add advertising, we’ll update this page and ask for your consent first.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and collection, message us through Instagram or YouTube from the email you signed in with, and we’ll remove it.
